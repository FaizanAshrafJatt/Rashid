A proposal to work together

Let's grow your businesses together, as partners.

We've thought carefully about how we can work together on your businesses, and we'd like to do it as partners.

You own the companies, bring the clients and lead the commercial side. We build and run the operation behind them here: the office, the people and the day-to-day work. Our reward comes from the growth we create together, so both of us are working toward the same result.

This page sets out how we see it working. It's a starting point for us to sit down and shape it together.

Business by business

How we'd work on each one

Ecommerce and IT work the same way, with a share of profit. Property and law run differently, so for those we'd like to agree an approach with you.

Ecommerce

Share of net profit

We run the operation: the store, products, ads, content, customer service and reporting. The numbers are open to both of us at any time. After the business covers its costs, we share the profit that's left.

IT services

Share of net profit

Your UK company is the face of the IT business. Clients sign with your company and pay your company, so the contracts, the client relationships and the commercial risk all sit with you. Your office here does the work, and we run that office and its team. After costs, we share the profit.

Client→ Your UK company→ Your office here delivers→ Profit shared

Property and law firm

Your choice

The new team will support both businesses as well. These two work differently from ecommerce and IT, so we've set out the model we recommend and a few alternatives. You can choose a different one for each business, or adjust as the partnership grows.

Recommended What we prefer

A management fee plus a share of the profit

This is the model we'd most like to work with on property and the law firm. It has two parts.

Part one

A smaller monthly management fee

This covers our management only: running the operation, leading the team, planning the work and reporting to you. It is not for the team. The team's salaries are part of your investment in the office and are covered separately.

Part two

A share of the profit as our reward

On top of the management fee, we take a share of the profit the business makes. This is our reward for growing it, so we only earn more when you do.

Fully transparent, on both sides

  • You see exactly what the management fee covers, and the team costs are shown separately.
  • We agree together, in writing, what counts as profit before anything is shared.
  • Both of us see the same numbers, every month.

Why we prefer it: the fee keeps the management steady through quieter months, and the profit share keeps us focused on growth. Neither side carries all the risk.

If you'd rather work another way, we're open to these as well:

Other option

Profit share only

How it works
No management fee. We share in the profit the business makes, the same way as ecommerce and IT.
What it needs
Open numbers on both sides, and an agreed view of what counts as profit.
Other option

Management fee only

How it works
A set monthly fee for managing the operation, with no profit share.
What you keep
All of the business's profit stays with you.
Other option

Share per deal or new client

How it works
We earn each time a property sale completes, or each time the firm takes on a new client.
What you get
Our reward is tied to clear, countable results.
Other option

Partner on specific projects

How it works
We share in the results of particular projects we run together, such as a property we take to sale or a new service line for the firm.
What you get
The rest of the business stays entirely yours.

We're not looking for a short-term arrangement. We want to grow these businesses with you, and grow alongside them.
